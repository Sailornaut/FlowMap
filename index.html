Before we start

A clear fit
makes a better pilot.

Will this send messages to my customers?

The pilot organizes follow-ups for your team. Customer messages remain a human action. The optional digest goes only to an internal address you choose.

What if my inquiries arrive somewhere else?

Email, CRM, and other sources may need a different scope. Describe the source first so we can check feasibility and price before you commit.

How will we know it works?

We test agreed sample inquiries, date rules, ownership, and the output together. Acceptance means those checks pass; it does not promise a particular sales result.

What does my team need to maintain?

Keep status, owner, and the last contact date current. The workflow uses those fields to decide what needs attention. You receive the instructions and source when we hand it over.
